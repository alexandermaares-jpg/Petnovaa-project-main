OUR STORY

At Pet Nova we believe that the best ideas are born from teamwork. Our team shares a common vision: to develop innovative solutions that improve the lives of pets and strengthen the bond with their families. Every member contributes creativity, commitment, and passion to make this project a reality for every single pet owner out there.
