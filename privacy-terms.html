Privacy & Terms

How PETNOVA handles information, and the rules for using this prototype.

Last updated: November 2, 2026

PETNOVA is a community-focused prototype built to help pet families in Panama organize care, explore wellness information, and connect with one another. This page explains what information the prototype handles, how it is stored, and the terms that apply when you use it. Because PETNOVA is a demonstration, these terms describe the current behavior of the site and are not a substitute for professional legal advice.

Privacy Policy

1. Overview

PETNOVA is designed to keep your information on your own device. The prototype does not operate a backend server that collects, stores, or sells personal data. Most of the information you enter is saved in your browser's local storage and remains under your control.

2. Information we handle

Pet profiles. When you add a pet in "Mi PETNOVA", the prototype stores the pet's name, species, optional birth date, and optional notes. This information is saved in your browser's local storage under the key "petnova-pets".

Reminders. Care reminders you create (title, date, and optional pet) are saved in your browser's local storage under the key "petnova-reminders".

Interest selections. On the Contact page, your chosen topic of interest is saved in your browser's local storage under the key "petnova-interests", together with the date it was saved.

Sign-in details. The sign-in and sign-up forms in this prototype are not connected to a real authentication service. Email addresses and passwords you type are not transmitted or stored by PETNOVA. Any data entered there exists only in the page and is discarded when you leave.

Technical data. Like most websites, the hosting provider may automatically receive standard request information such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you request. PETNOVA itself does not add analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts beyond the assets required to render the site.

3. How we use information

Information saved in your browser is used only to display your pets, reminders, and interests back to you within PETNOVA. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond the features you interact with. Because there is no PETNOVA server involved, no one at PETNOVA can view the data you store locally.

4. Storage and security

All pet, reminder, and interest data lives in your browser's local storage. It stays on your device and is not sent over the network to PETNOVA. You are responsible for the security of your own device and browser. We recommend not entering sensitive personal information, medical details about people, or anything you would not want stored on a shared or public device.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

PETNOVA does not set tracking cookies or use third-party analytics. The site may rely on standard browser features (such as local storage) to remember your entries. You can clear this data at any time through your browser settings.

6. Your choices and rights

You can remove all locally stored PETNOVA data by clearing this site's local storage (and, if applicable, site data) in your browser settings. You can also delete individual pets or reminders from within the prototype. If you believe any information about you has been handled in error, please contact us using the details below.

7. Children's privacy

PETNOVA is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through this site, please contact us so we can help remove it from the local device.

8. Third parties

The prototype loads fonts from Google Fonts and, on the home page, map tiles and a map library from unpkg. Those services may receive standard request information as part of delivering their resources. PETNOVA does not share your pet profiles, reminders, or interests with any third party.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the prototype evolves. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of PETNOVA after an update means you accept the revised policy.
