Privacy & Terms

How this prototype handles information and how to use it.

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Privacy

Information you enter

This prototype stores pet profiles and reminders in your browser's local storage. That information stays in the browser on your device; this site does not send it to a PETNOVA server.

Your choices

You can remove saved profiles and reminders by clearing this site's local storage in your browser settings. Avoid entering sensitive personal information.

Interest form

The interest form is a local demonstration. It does not send your response to PETNOVA or a third party.

Terms

Prototype use

PETNOVA is presented as an informational prototype. Features may change and are provided for demonstration purposes.

Not veterinary advice

Content on this site is general information. It is not a substitute for diagnosis or treatment from a qualified veterinary professional.

Responsible use

Use the tools as organizational aids and confirm important health decisions with your veterinarian. You are responsible for maintaining your own records and reminders.

These pages describe the current prototype behavior and are not a substitute for legal advice.
